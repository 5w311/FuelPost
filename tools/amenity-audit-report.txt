  Three fields the brief asked for cannot be fully collected from TA's site.
  These are properties of the source, confirmed against raw HTML, not parser bugs:

  1. SHOWER COUNTS ARE NOT PUBLISHED. TA location pages carry no shower
     number anywhere. The only mention is boilerplate prose ("use our laundry
     and shower facilities"). DATA's shower column cannot be audited against
     this source at all. The hand check that produced the known-answer shower
     figures must have used another source.

     fetched in this run mentions a walking trail. DATA carries W on many rows.
     Every one of those therefore reports as "in DATA, not on TA" below. That is
     one systematic source difference, NOT that many independent data errors,
     and it should be decided as a single question: did TA retire the amenity,
     or retire the listing?

     brands than the by-hand spot check found — including TA's own Country
     Pride, absent from the Baldwin page. Treat the brand lists below as a
     lower bound on what is actually at each stop.

PROVENANCE
  DATA's stations come from the Covenant fuel book Rev 01-2026, and that book
  governs which stops are in network. TA's site governs what is currently at
  those stops. Where they disagree about amenities the site is more current;
  where they disagree about a station existing, the book wins and the row stays.
  This report states disagreements. It does not resolve them, and it neither
  removed nor added a row.
